### Opt-in synthetic dictation, 7 November

test_dictation.py used real English Gboard/Return and two Windows Zira fixtures,
48 kHz mono S16 PCM over authenticated emulator gRPC. Host microphone stayed
disabled; guest audio permission was restored. The scoped allowlist exposed only
microphone-state inspection and audio injection.

| Recognized | Saved |
| --- | --- |
| Two cartons of milk | Same text plus one terminal ASCII space |
| Bread, 6 rolls | Same text plus one terminal ASCII space |

The driver accepts only equality or that single extra space, with no word trimming.
Its origin was not proved. A real Space-tap control committed the second string
before Return and then matched exactly. Ten records passed, including one-row
submission, cleared draft, retained Gboard and exact cold persistence.

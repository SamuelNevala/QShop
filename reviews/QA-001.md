## Real dictation

### Synthetic English Gboard run (2026-11-07)

`tests/android/test_dictation.py` drives the real Gboard voice button and native
Return on emulator-5580. Two offline Windows Zira speech fixtures were streamed
as paced 48 kHz mono S16 PCM through authenticated emulator gRPC. The task
allowlist exposes only microphone-state inspection and audio injection;
`realAudioEnabled` was false before/after injection and at completion. No host
microphone was enabled or captured. Gboard's guest audio permission was granted
for this test and restored afterward.

| Spoken fixture | Recognized before submission | Saved text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two cartons of milk | `Two cartons of milk` | `Two cartons of milk ` (one terminal space) |
| Bread comma six rolls | `Bread, 6 rolls` | `Bread, 6 rolls ` |

Direct Return produced one terminal ASCII space at the IME commit boundary.
The driver records that exact difference; it accepts only equality or that one
additional space, without trimming/normalizing words. Its origin is not proved
by this run. As a separate control, a real Gboard Space tap committed the second
entry to `Bread, 6 rolls ` before Return; the saved string then matched exactly.
Both submissions create exactly one row, clear the draft and retain Gboard.
Cold restart preserves exact names, identities, order and check flags. All ten
records pass in `out/displays/android-dictation-verified/results.json`, including
exact expected-transcript assertions for both WAVs and independent cleanup.

`test_dictation_control.py` checks key cleanup when channel creation/closure
fails and all-resource cleanup after an ADB disconnect. All three fail against
the earlier driver snapshot and pass after the review corrections; reports are
`out/displays/dictation-control-before.txt` and `dictation-control-after.txt`.

An earlier blocking-stream prototype issued nested microphone-state RPCs and
stalled the emulator. Its logs remain in `out/displays/voice-ui-third.txt`; the
verified task-owned emulator was restarted. The final driver uses paced audio
and state checks outside the stream. This was not established as an app defect.

This establishes synthetic speech through the real recognizer/IME/app path.
Natural speech, correction/cancellation of dictated words and one-handed shop
use remain separate physical acceptance checks below. Ordinary entry tests
continue to avoid speech; the audio test is explicit and opt-in.

1. Dictate two different entries using the normal language, including quantities
   and punctuation. Record the actual recognized text before submission.
2. Press the keyboard's Return after each. Verify one entry per submission, no
   lost/duplicated text, and a keyboard that remains open for the next entry.
3. Correct a recognized word and cancel an unfinished composition/replacement.
   Confirm that intentional edits survive and cancelled original items return.
4. Hide/reopen the keyboard and open/close the drawer with unfinished text.
   Verify the draft follows the documented Back/editor policy.
5. Restart and compare saved entries with the submitted text. Distinguish speech
   recognition errors from text the app loses or changes after recognition.

## Back confirmation (UI-006)

On the affected Pixel, perform one edge gesture per step: close the drawer;
with editing/keyboard open hide only the keyboard; with the keyboard closed leave
edit mode; in shopping mode background the app. Return and check saved contents.
Hiding the keyboard preserves the draft. Leaving edit mode discards unfinished
input and restores the original item if a replacement was pending. About and
selector popups close before the drawer. Record both edges where practical.

# UX-003: custom Material color roles

Reviewed/implemented against c632dd3 and Qt 6.12.0; user accepted 7 November 2026.
**Keep all 19 families, vivid ramps, neutral surfaces and System/Light/Dark.**
Custom controls can use Material attached roles; no token framework, extra picker
or component replacement is needed. Geometry/gesture ownership stays unchanged.

## Regression evidence

[UI-029](../tickets/UI-029.json) and [UI-030](../tickets/UI-030.json) retain
original-source negative controls, exact tests and before/after results.
tests/customcolors/tst_customcolors.qml covers all families, Light/Dark and
native Windows System, actual ink over photographic extremes, real editor presses,
focus, drawer propagation, tabs and thumb. Controls persist through live changes.
Android test_customcolors.py uses actual held/pressed controls and same-frame
neighbor pixels to avoid moving-photo reference races.

At acceptance: 17 Windows suites/1,622 records passed, including 268 offscreen
and 282 native custom-color records; 283 Android checks passed (48 custom color,
6 cleanup, 24 recovery, 26 About, 42 popup, 137 System/safe-area). Both APK
package/signature checks, 67 QML/11 C++ formatting and both analyzers passed.
QML lint exited zero with five existing warnings recorded in the tickets.

Targets: 4.5:1 small text, 3:1 meaningful graphics after composition. Decorative,
disabled and hypothetical unused variants do not justify blanket recoloring.
Outdoor viewing and live Android correction/user recognition remain unproven.

# UX-001: primary and accent review

Reviewed 5–6 November 2026, Qt 6.12.0, baseline cf0fb87. **Keep the vivid shade
shift, 19 families and two user choices.** Fix individual foreground/container
pairings; do not replace the palette with a muted theme. The review itself changed
no app behavior. Follow-up defects UI-025/UI-026 are now resolved.

## Role decisions

| Area | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Theme/window | Keep central resolved theme/primary/accent and neutral surfaces |
| Weekdays | Primary identifies today; treat the small name as ink and the circle as a fill with its own readable foreground |
| Theme buttons | Accent marks selection; icon needs contrast against its fill; caption stays neutral |
| Selectors/slider/tabs | Vivid swatches/fills are useful; selected text/focus cues need readable ink/boundaries |
| Undo | Preserve normal TextItem foreground; choose a compatible fill and solid normal surface rather than duplicate clipped text |
| Pulley/editor/custom controls | Local role audit belongs to UX-003; retain action/state ownership |
| Check/delete | Red/green meaning belongs to UX-002; no new picker |

Qt's primaryTextColor is a neutral text role. In this kit,
primaryHighlightedTextColor is white, not an M3 contrast-aware onPrimary token.
QShopper has no main toolbar; its primary role is principally today's indication.

## Intentional shade mapping

| Role | Stock Qt Light / Dark | App Light / Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | 500 / 500 | 500 / 200 |
| Chromatic accent | 500 / 200 | A400 / A200 |
| Brown/Grey/BlueGrey accent | 500 / 200 | 500 / 200 |

This selects existing ramps, not an arbitrary color-space offset. Green becomes
more mint-like; neutral families fall back because they lack A ramps. Lighter
Dark primary improves today's name. Removing the shift does not generally fix
white-on-fill contrast. Swatches matched all 19 resolved families.

Qt Material is not Android's current M3/Expressive system: no automatic dynamic
wallpaper palette, harmonized tonal scheme or contrast-aware role pairs are
claimed. Independent color-family selection is acceptable here because content
surfaces stay neutral; check actual pairs rather than restricting combinations.

## Baseline evidence

Native Windows production-component probes collected 92 palette records
(19 families plus four mixed pairs, Light/Dark and both resolved System modes).
Of 19 families, baseline day numbers missed 4.5:1 in 15 Light/19 Dark cases;
selected icons missed 3:1 in 10/13; full-fill Undo text missed 4.5:1 in 4/18.
Default Dark Undo measured 1.43:1. Sixty composed Undo frames across ten artworks
showed that the bright full fill, rather than the uncovered photo, caused that failure.

The final supported offscreen collection passed 24 records, matching native
palette values. A full offscreen attempt failed its unsupported System override
(25 pass/1 fail); it is not native System evidence. Production drawer/software
composition differs from the isolated native fixture, so exact ratios are
specific to their retained frames. A passing measurement probe is not a contrast pass.

QShopper privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who is responsible

QShopper is developed by Samuel Nevala. For support or privacy enquiries, contact samuel.nevala@gmail.com. This policy covers the official Android app and explains the separate services you may choose to use with it.

Your shopping data

Item names, shopping lists, their order and checked state are saved in a database in the app's private storage. Preferences such as theme, colors and swipe sensitivity are saved on the device. QShopper uses these records to show and reuse your lists. It has no QShopper account, developer-operated cloud sync, advertising, tracking SDK or analytics service. The app does not transmit your shopping lists to the developer.

Android backup

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device, Android version, account and backup settings, the operating system may back up app data and preferences to your backup provider and restore or transfer them to another device. This happens under the operating system/provider's controls and privacy terms. The developer cannot read or manage those backups. Review your device's backup settings if you do not want app data included.

Keyboard and voice typing

Typing is handled by your selected Android keyboard. Voice typing, if you choose it, is provided by that keyboard rather than by a QShopper microphone feature. The keyboard or its speech provider may process typed text, speech or recordings according to its own settings and privacy policy, including sending data off the device. QShopper receives the text the keyboard inserts into the editor. It does not request microphone, camera, contacts or location access.

Google Play purchases and diagnostics

Google Play handles payment for the official app under Google's terms and privacy policy. The developer may access order and transaction information through Google's seller tools to manage purchases, refunds and accounting. QShopper itself does not ask for payment-card details or include an in-app payment service.

Android and Google Play may provide system crash, app-not-responding, compatibility and store reports to the developer. QShopper does not add a separate crash-reporting or analytics SDK. Information supplied by these platform services is handled under their terms and settings.

Contacting support

If you email the developer, the developer receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request, investigate problems or handle purchases and legal obligations. Do not send shopping lists or other personal information you do not want to share. Correspondence and order records are kept only as long as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations; they are not used for advertising.

Storage, retention and deletion

Your active shopping data remains in the app until you remove it or clear the app's storage. Deletion actions may remain recoverable through the app's temporary Undo feature until recovery expires or ends. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the local app data through Android. It does not guarantee immediate deletion of older operating-system backups; manage those through your backup provider. The developer has no remote copy of your shopping lists to delete.

Android protects the app's private storage using its platform security. QShopper does not add its own database encryption or claim to provide secure erasure. Device backups, your keyboard and your email provider have their own security arrangements.

Your privacy requests

You can ask the developer about personal information held in support correspondence or order records, and request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Applicable law may also provide rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of information, or complain to a data-protection authority. Some transaction records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations. Local shopping data is controlled on your device as described above.

Website and source pages

The public policy and source project are hosted by GitHub. Visiting those pages through a browser may provide connection and browser information to GitHub under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Reading this bundled policy inside QShopper does not open a website or contact GitHub.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if QShopper's data handling changes. The bundled policy describes that app version; the public policy shows the latest published version. Questions and privacy requests: samuel.nevala@gmail.com.
